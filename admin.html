What this panel controls

Register businesses and users happen automatically through sign-up; from here you can review who has joined, remove accounts or listings that break marketplace rules, monitor traffic and growth, generate reports, read feedback from users and businesses, and set the MTN MoMo / Mastercard accounts that collect business subscription payments.
